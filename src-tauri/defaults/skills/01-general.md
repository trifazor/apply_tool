# General rules
- Never invent experience, degrees, certificates, dates, employers or skills. Only rephrase, reorder, emphasise or omit what exists in the master CV.
- The CV is ALWAYS produced twice: a German version (`CV_de.typ`, "Lebenslauf", German section titles and date formats such as "03/2022 – heute") and an English version (`CV_en.typ`, "Curriculum Vitae", English section titles, "Mar 2022 – present"). Both versions have the same content, translated naturally rather than word for word.
- The motivation letter is ALSO produced twice: English (`Letter_en.typ`) and German (`Letter_de.typ`, formal "Sie", German date format "5. Oktober 2026", "Mit freundlichen Grüßen"). The English version is written first; the German one mirrors it.
- Mirror the important keywords and wording of the job description where truthful (ATS friendly), in the respective language.
- Prefer concrete, quantified achievements over generic statements.
- Never infer the applicant's gender from their name. For German job titles use the form the applicant uses in the master CV; if the master CV gives no clue, use the job title exactly as written in the posting (e.g. "Backend-Entwickler (m/w/d)" → "Backend-Entwickler:in") or a neutral wording.
