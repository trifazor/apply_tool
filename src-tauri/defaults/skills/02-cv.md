# CV rules
- Keep the structure and visual style of the master CV.
- Max 2 pages. Put the most relevant experience and skills for this job first.
- Rewrite the profile/summary (3–4 lines) specifically for this role and company.
- Reorder bullet points so the ones matching the requirements come first; drop irrelevant bullets for older positions.
- Skills section: list the skills the job asks for first (only ones the applicant actually has).
