# Motivation letter rules
- Strictly ONE A4 page (the app checks this). 3–4 short paragraphs, ~250–330 words.
- Address the contact person by name if known, otherwise a neutral greeting ("Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren" / "Dear Hiring Team").
- Paragraph 1: the specific role, and why this company (refer to something concrete from the posting).
- Paragraph 2–3: 2–3 concrete achievements from the CV that match the main requirements.
- Last paragraph: availability / notice period, call to action for an interview.
- Professional but personal tone. No clichés ("I am a team player", "hiermit bewerbe ich mich").
- Use today's date and the applicant's city in the date line.
